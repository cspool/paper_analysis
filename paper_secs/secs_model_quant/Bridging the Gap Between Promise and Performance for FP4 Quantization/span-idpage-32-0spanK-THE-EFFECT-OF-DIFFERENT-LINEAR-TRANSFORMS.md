# <span id="page-32-0"></span>K THE EFFECT OF DIFFERENT LINEAR TRANSFORMS

In this section we ablate various choices of transforms adopted for outlier mitigation of outliers. Specifically, we consider the following options:

- Identity transform.
- Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT).
- Hadamard rotation [\[50;](#page-13-1) [4;](#page-10-4) [11\]](#page-11-15).
- Grouped Sequency-arranged Rotation (GSR) [\[8\]](#page-10-9).

We sweep over different options of transform sizes ({16, 32, 64, 128, 256}) both for NVFP and MXFP formats. The average score on 5 tasks from LM Evaluation Harness (piqa, winogrande, hellaswag, arc-easy, arc-challenge) is reported.

From these results, one can observe that rotations yield small improvement relative to identity transform for MXFP format and minor degradation for NVFP with RTN quantization. Different transform sizes perform more or less the same.
