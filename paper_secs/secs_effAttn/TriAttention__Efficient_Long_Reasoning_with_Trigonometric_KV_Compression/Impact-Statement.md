# Impact Statement

This paper advances inference-time reasoning efficiency in LLMs. The proposed TriAttention cuts computational cost and latency without compromising accuracy, enabling more efficient, accessible deployment of reasoning-capable LLMs. Its societal and ethical implications align with standard ML efficiency improvements—including sustainability and usability benefits—and it introduces no new model capabilities, data sources, or application domains, thus raising no novel ethical concerns beyond those already established for LLMs, with no further broader impacts.
