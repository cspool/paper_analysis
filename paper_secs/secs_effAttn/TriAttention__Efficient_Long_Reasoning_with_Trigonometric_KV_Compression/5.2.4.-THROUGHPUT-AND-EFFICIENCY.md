# 5.2.4. THROUGHPUT AND EFFICIENCY

We evaluate efficiency from two perspectives: comparison with Full Attention and with prior compression methods. Following the evaluation protocol of R-KV [\(Cai et al.,](#page-8-4) [2025\)](#page-8-4),

throughput is measured as the average tokens generated per second over 16K decoding length on a single A100 80GB GPU at maximum batch size.

Comparison with Full Attention. At comparable accuracy, TriAttention achieves substantial efficiency gains (Figure [1,](#page-0-0) Table [4\)](#page-7-1). On AIME25, at identical accuracy to Full Attention (40.8%), TriAttention achieves 2.5× higher throughput or 10.7× KV memory reduction. On MATH 500, the throughput gain reaches 6.3× (1,405 vs. 223 tokens/s) while maintaining comparable accuracy (68.4% vs. 69.6%).

Comparison with R-KV. Table [5](#page-7-2) compares TriAttention with R-KV under two settings. At comparable accuracy, Tri-Attention requires only half the KV budget (1,024 vs. 2,048 tokens) while achieving 85% higher throughput (1,405 vs. 760 tokens/s). At the same memory budget, TriAttention maintains similar throughput but achieves higher accuracy— 8% on MATH 500 and 15% on AIME24. TriAttention provides a strictly better accuracy-efficiency trade-off.
