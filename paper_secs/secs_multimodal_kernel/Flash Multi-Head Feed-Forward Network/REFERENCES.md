## <span id="page-12-0"></span>A TRITON PSEUDO CODE FOR SRAMFFN

Triton code can run efficiently on consumer grade GPUs (like RTX3090) but not on Hopper cards.

## <span id="page-12-1"></span>Algorithm 1 SRAMFFN-FORWARD-TRITON

```
Require: Q \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times H \times L \times d_h}, K, U, V \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times E \times d_e \times d_h}, R \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times H \times L \times E}
      Strategy: Parallelize over batch, head, and sequence blocks. Inner loops iterate over sub-
      networks and intermediate dimension blocks.
      Meta: BLOCK_SEQ, BLOCK_INTER, HEAD_DIM= d_h, PFFN_DIM= d_e, NUM_PFFN= E
      Grid: pid0\leftarrowseq_block, pid1\leftarrowhead h, pid2\leftarrowbatch b
 1: s_0 \leftarrow pid0 \cdot BLOCK\_SEQ
 2: Q_{\text{blk}} \leftarrow Q[b, h, s_0 : s_0 + \text{BLOCK\_SEQ}, :]
3: O_{\text{acc}} \leftarrow \mathbf{0}^{\text{BLOCK\_SEQ} \times d_h}
                                                                                                              ⊳ masked at sequence tail
                                                                                                                   > SRAM accumulator
 4: for e = 0 to E - 1 do
            R_{\text{rows}} \leftarrow R[b, h, s_0 : s_0 + \text{BLOCK\_SEQ}, e]
 5:
           for m=0 to d_e{-1} step <code>BLOCK_INTER</code> do
 6:
                 K_{\text{tile}} \leftarrow K[h, e, m:m+\text{BLOCK\_INTER},:]^{\top}
 7:
 8:
                 U_{\text{tile}} \leftarrow U[h, e, m:m+\text{BLOCK\_INTER}, :]
 9:
                 V_{\text{tile}} \leftarrow V[h, e, m:m+\text{BLOCK\_INTER},:]
10:
                 M \leftarrow Q_{\text{blk}} \cdot K_{\text{tile}}; \quad N \leftarrow Q_{\text{blk}} \cdot U_{\text{tile}}
                 A \leftarrow \text{SiLU}(M) \odot N; \quad A \leftarrow A \odot R_{\text{rows}}
11:
12.
                 O_{\rm acc} \leftarrow O_{\rm acc} + A \cdot V_{\rm tile}
           end for
13:
14: end for
15: O[b, h, s_0 : s_0 + \text{BLOCK\_SEQ}, :] \leftarrow O_{\text{acc}}
```
