# 8 Conclusion

This paper proposes Tetris, a serving system empowered by chunkwise dynamic sequence parallelism (CDSP) for online long-context LLM serving. CDSP's fine-grained SP allocation satisfies diverse resource demands while maximizing resource utilization. With the load-aware scheduling, Tetris fully unveils CDSP's benefits under dynamic online workloads. Experiments on real-world workloads shows that Tetris achieves up to 4.35× lower TTFT than existing SOTA systems and increases max serving capacity by up to 45%.
