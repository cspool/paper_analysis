# <span id="page-17-1"></span>F Generalization to Additional Benchmarks

As shown in Section [4.2,](#page-4-1) our existing evaluation spans across in- and out-of-domain commonsense, mathematical, and symbolic reasoning. To further investigate the generalizability of ARM, we extend the evaluation to two additional benchmarks: *1)* GPQA [\[37\]](#page-11-14), a challenging QA dataset designed to test compositional reasoning, and *2)* StrategyQA [\[8\]](#page-9-12), a benchmark of yes/no questions that require implicit multi-hop reasoning.

As shown in Table [6,](#page-18-1) ARM achieves comparable accuracy to the GRPO baseline while significantly reducing token cost by an average of 50%, and up to 65% in StrategyQA, consistent with our main findings discussed in Section [4.3.](#page-5-0) This demonstrates that ARM's adaptive reasoning behavior generalizes well to more tasks.
