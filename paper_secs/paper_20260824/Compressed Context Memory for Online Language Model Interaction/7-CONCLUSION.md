# 7 CONCLUSION

We present a novel compressed context memory system that dynamically compresses contextual information, thereby enhancing the online inference efficiency of language models. To ensure efficient training, we develop a parallelized training strategy and introduce a conditional adapter. Our approach achieves reduced memory and attention FLOPS complexities compared to previous fixedcontext compression methods. We validate the practical applicability of our approach through a comprehensive evaluation on multi-task learning, personalization, and conversation applications.
