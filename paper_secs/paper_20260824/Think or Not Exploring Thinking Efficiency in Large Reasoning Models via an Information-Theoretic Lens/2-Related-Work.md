# 2 Related Work

Information-Theoretic Perspectives Information theory has long served as a lens for analyzing machine learning systems, particularly in studying generalization bounds [\[Russo and Zou,](#page-11-4) [2016,](#page-11-4) [Xu and Raginsky,](#page-12-1) [2017\]](#page-12-1) and understanding learning objectives [\[Slonim et al.,](#page-11-5) [2002\]](#page-11-5). Recent work extends these ideas to LLMs, using entropy-based measures to evaluate reasoning reliability [\[Ton](#page-12-2) [et al.,](#page-12-2) [2024,](#page-12-2) [Gan et al.,](#page-9-2) [2025\]](#page-9-2). Semantic entropy, in particular, has been proposed as a tool for detecting hallucinations by measuring variability in meaning across generations [\[Farquhar et al.,](#page-9-3) [2024\]](#page-9-3), and can be efficiently estimated using hidden states alone [\[Kossen et al.,](#page-10-5) [2024\]](#page-10-5). Other approaches use entropy to identify reasoning failures in multi-step generation without requiring supervision [\[Ali et al.,](#page-9-4) [2025\]](#page-9-4).

Adaptive and Efficient Reasoning Efficiency in LLMs is an active area of research, with methods that adapt step counts based on task difficulty, confidence, or resource constraints [\[Han et al.,](#page-10-6) [2024,](#page-10-6) [Pan et al.,](#page-10-7) [2024,](#page-10-7) [Shen et al.,](#page-11-6) [2025\]](#page-11-6). Early exit mechanisms and dynamic token allocation [\[Yang et al.,](#page-12-3) [2025a,](#page-12-3) [Qu et al.,](#page-11-7) [2025\]](#page-11-7) aim to reduce unnecessary computation, while approaches such as elastic CoT and multi-scale reasoning seek to better align model capacity with problem complexity [\[Ma](#page-10-8) [et al.,](#page-10-8) [2025b,](#page-10-8) [Kirchner et al.,](#page-10-9) [2024\]](#page-10-9). Studies have shown that longer CoT do not always improve performance [\[Wu et al.,](#page-12-4) [2025,](#page-12-4) [Yang et al.,](#page-12-5) [2025b\]](#page-12-5), and in some cases can lead to overthinking, particularly in high-capacity models [\[Chen et al.,](#page-9-5) [2024\]](#page-9-5). This has led to interest in minimal or even implicit reasoning strategies [\[Ma et al.,](#page-10-10) [2025a,](#page-10-10) [Sui et al.,](#page-11-8) [2025\]](#page-11-8), emphasizing the need for more nuanced reasoning strategies and adaptive control over reasoning depth.
