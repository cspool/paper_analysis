# 2. Background and Related Work

In this section, we explore Large Reasoning Models (LRMs) and agentic environments, where LRMs must balance sophisticated reasoning with practical actions. We examine how these models navigate environments requiring deep analytical thinking and concrete interactions. This leads to a fundamental dilemma between reasoning depth and action—a tension we will explore in [Section 3.](#page-2-0)

## 2.1. Large Reasoning Models and Agentic Environments

LRMs, defined as language models optimized through process reward models and test-time compute scaling [\(Xu](#page-11-0) [et al.,](#page-11-0) [2025\)](#page-11-0), represent an evolution beyond traditional LLMs through their focus on reliable step-by-step reasoning [\(Guo et al.,](#page-9-1) [2025;](#page-9-1) [OpenAI,](#page-10-9) [2025c\)](#page-10-9). These models achieve unprecedented performance through extended chainof-thought reasoning [\(Wei et al.,](#page-10-10) [2023\)](#page-10-10) and rigorous selfverification [\(Madaan et al.,](#page-9-6) [2023\)](#page-9-6). However, their extended focus on internal reasoning depth raises important questions about performance in interactive environments.

Agency in AI Systems While traditional AI defined agents broadly as entities that perceive and act upon their environment [\(Russell & Norvig,](#page-10-11) [1995\)](#page-10-11), modern approaches view agency as a spectrum of capabilities [\(Zhang et al.,](#page-11-1) [2024;](#page-11-1) [Kapoor et al.,](#page-9-7) [2024\)](#page-9-7), emphasizing autonomous goal pursuit, natural language interfaces, and structured outputs like tool use [\(Yang et al.,](#page-11-2) [2024b\)](#page-11-2). This framework has been particularly influential in software engineering, where various agent architectures [\(Research,](#page-10-12) [2024;](#page-10-12) [Blog,](#page-9-8) [2024;](#page-9-8) [Liu et al.,](#page-9-9) [2024\)](#page-9-9) have been developed to solve real-world GitHub issues [\(Jimenez et al.,](#page-9-5) [2024\)](#page-9-5). Our work examines how LRMs' distinctive reasoning capabilities affect their performance in these agentic environments.
