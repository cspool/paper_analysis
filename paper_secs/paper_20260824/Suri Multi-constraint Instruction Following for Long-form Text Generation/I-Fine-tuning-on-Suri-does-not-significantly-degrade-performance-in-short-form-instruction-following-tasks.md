# I Fine-tuning on **Suri** does not significantly degrade performance in short-form instruction following tasks

We measure the performance of Suri-I-ORPO and Suri-SFT on popular benchmarks using lm-evaluationharness [\(Gao et al.,](#page-9-19) [2024\)](#page-9-19). Our findings indicate that our fine-tuned models do not significantly degrade the performance of the baseline instruct model (Table [19\)](#page-31-2). In fact, they slightly improve performance on most benchmarks, with the exceptions of HellaSwag, WinoGrande, and Arc-challenge.
