# 2. Related Work

To our knowledge, we offer the first technical characterization of how agents running in production are built. Additional related work in each category appears in [§F.](#page-29-0)

Commercial Agent Surveys. Prior work examines agent adoption from adjacent perspectives. [Challapally et al.](#page-9-0) [\(2025\)](#page-9-0) study economic viability from business executives' views at companies attempting agent integration. [Shome](#page-12-2) [et al.](#page-12-2) [\(2026\)](#page-12-2) analyze marketing materials and conduct user studies, revealing capability gaps. [LangChain](#page-10-3) [\(2024\)](#page-10-3) surveyed 1,300 professionals on motivations and challenges. And various consulting reports focus on organizational readiness [\(PwC,](#page-12-3) [2025;](#page-12-3) [Sukharevsky et al.,](#page-12-4) [2025\)](#page-12-4). Our work differs in scope (production systems) and focus (engineeringlevel technical data from practitioners).

Research Literature Surveys. Academic surveys synthesize published research, providing overviews of agent architectures and taxonomies [\(Wang et al.,](#page-13-0) [2024b;](#page-13-0) [Liu et al.,](#page-10-4) [2025\)](#page-10-4), with specialized surveys on evaluation [\(Yehudai et al.,](#page-13-2) [2025\)](#page-13-2) and security [\(He et al.,](#page-10-5) [2025\)](#page-10-5). In contrast, our empirical study collects primary data directly from practitioners building deployed agents through original interviews and surveys rather than synthesizing existing literature.

Single-System Studies. Companies publish on individual systems [\(Gottweis et al.,](#page-9-1) [2025;](#page-9-1) [Anthropic,](#page-9-2) [2024;](#page-9-2) [Jackson](#page-10-6) [et al.,](#page-10-6) [2025\)](#page-10-6) and open-source implementations [\(OpenAI,](#page-11-2) [2025a;](#page-11-2) [OpenHands,](#page-11-3) [2025\)](#page-11-3), providing valuable insights into specific systems. We report common patterns across diverse deployments rather than documenting individual systems.
