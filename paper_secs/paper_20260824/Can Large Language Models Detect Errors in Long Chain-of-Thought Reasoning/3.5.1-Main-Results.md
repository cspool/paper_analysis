# 3.5.1 Main Results

In Table [2,](#page-9-2) we provide the results of different LLMs on DeltaBench. For PRMs, we have the following observations: (1). Existing PRMs usually achieve low performance, which indicates that existing PRMs cannot identify the errors in long CoTs effectively and it is necessary to improve the performance of PRMs. (2). Larger PRMs do not lead to better performance. For example, the Qwen2.5-Math-PRM-72B is inferior to wen2.5-Math-PRM-7B. For critic models, we have the following findings: (1) GPT-4-turbo-128k archives the best critique results, which is better than other models (e.g., GPT-4o) a lot in DeltaBench. (2) For o1-like models (e.g., DeepSeek-R1, o1-mini, o1-preview), we observe that the results of these models are not superior to non-o1-like models, with the performance of o1-preview is even lower than Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct. A detailed analysis of underperforming models is provided in Appendix [C.](#page-24-2)
