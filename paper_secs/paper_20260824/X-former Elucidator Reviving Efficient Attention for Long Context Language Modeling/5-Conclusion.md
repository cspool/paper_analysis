# 5 Conclusion

The long sequence modeling ability of LLMs is becoming increasingly important in real-world AI applications from various felds. Emerging "X-former" approaches provide suffcient effciency improvement opportunities but also bring unprecedented model selection challenges. We revisited the recent advances of long-context LLMs and provided a new taxonomy of these approaches and their origins. We selected twelve representative effcient attention mechanisms and provided a comprehensive evaluation and in-depth analysis to study their effectiveness and effciencies. Based on these results, we summarized our fndings and proposed several key insights to promote the follow-up research for both data scientists and system engineers aiming for developing LLMs.
