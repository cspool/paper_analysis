# **7. Conclusion**

Our work identifies a fundamental limitation of current test-time compute strategies, where sequential reasoning gets caught in Tunnel Vision set by its own initial tokens. We demonstrate that this is not an inherent model capability limit but a failure of the scaling strategy itself. We then introduce ParaThinker, a framework for native parallel reasoning that sidesteps these traps by generating and synthesizing multiple thought-paths simultaneously. This approach proves that scaling compute in parallel (width) is a more effective and efficient path to superior reasoning than simply scaling sequentially (depth). While our method is a significant first step, future work could explore more advanced aggregation strategies and deeper reinforcement learning. Fundamentally, our results argue that to unlock the next level of reasoning, LLM test-time compute should scale in parallel.
