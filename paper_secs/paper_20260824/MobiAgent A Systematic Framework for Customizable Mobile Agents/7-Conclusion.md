# 7 Conclusion

In this paper, we address the challenges of building, evaluating and deploying mobile agent by introducing a comprehensive, full-stack solution encompassing the MobiMind model series, the AgentRR acceleration framework, and the MobiFlow evaluation benchmark. Experiments show that MobiAgent achieves state-of-the-art task completion rates that surpass both general-purpose models like GPT-5 and Gemini-2.5 Pro, and other specialized mobile agents in the MobiFlow benchmark. Moreover, The experience replay mechanism of AgentRR framework delivers a 2-3x optimization on agent's task completion latency in real-world scenarios. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach in creating customizable and practical mobile agents.
