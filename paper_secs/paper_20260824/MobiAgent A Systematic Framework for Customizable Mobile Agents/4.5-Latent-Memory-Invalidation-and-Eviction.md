# 4.5 Latent Memory Invalidation and Eviction

Experiences may become obsolete as the environment changes, such as application updates or device replacements. Compared to high-level experiences, low-level experiences (e.g., action coordinates output by the Actor) tend to become outdated more frequently. In the AgentRR system, it is essential to promptly detect and update obsolete experiences to ensure the correctness of subsequent action reuse.
