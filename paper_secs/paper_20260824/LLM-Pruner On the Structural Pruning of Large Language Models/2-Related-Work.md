# 2 Related Work

Compression of Language Model. Language models [\[9,](#page-10-6) [29,](#page-11-6) [25\]](#page-11-7) have gained much attention and increase the need to reduce the size of parameters and reduce the latency [\[23,](#page-11-8) [46\]](#page-12-10). To compress the language model, previous works can be divided into several categories: network pruning [\[21,](#page-11-1) [61,](#page-13-6) [30,](#page-11-9) [15\]](#page-10-7), knowledge distillation [\[44,](#page-12-5) [45,](#page-12-7) [38\]](#page-12-11), quantization [\[63,](#page-13-7) [1,](#page-10-3) [66\]](#page-13-8) and other techniques, like early exit [\[60\]](#page-13-9) or dynamic token reduction [\[64\]](#page-13-10). We focus on the pruning of the language models, especially structural pruning [\[26\]](#page-11-10). Structural pruning removes the entire filter from the neural network, which is more hardware friendly. There are several ways to remove the structure, such as l1-dependent pruning [\[16,](#page-10-8) [67\]](#page-13-5), first-order importance estimation [\[18\]](#page-11-2), hessian-based estimation [\[21,](#page-11-1) [52\]](#page-12-12) or the optimal brain surgeon [\[24,](#page-11-11) [21\]](#page-11-1). As for the pruning unit in structural pruning, some works adopt the entire layer [\[10\]](#page-10-9) as the minimal unit, and others take the multi-head attention [\[50\]](#page-12-13) or the feed-forward layers [\[18,](#page-11-2) [34\]](#page-11-12) as the basic structure to prune. CoFi [\[59\]](#page-13-4) studies the pruning unit in different granularity.

Efficient and Low Resource Compression. With the growing size of models, there is an increasing demand for efficient LLM compression and compression is independent of the original training data. As for the efficient compression, [\[22\]](#page-11-5) accelerate the post-training by defining the reconstruction error as a linear least squares problem. [\[13,](#page-10-4) [12\]](#page-10-10) propose the layer-wise optimal brain surgeon. As for the constraint of availability of the training corpus, data-free pruning [\[43,](#page-12-14) [65\]](#page-13-11) come up with several strategies to prune the model by measuring neurons' similarity. Besides, [\[32,](#page-11-13) [31,](#page-11-14) [40\]](#page-12-15) proposes methods that distill the model without reliance on the training corpus of the model. However, those methods are too time-consuming, involving synthesizing samples by backpropagating the pre-trained language models.
