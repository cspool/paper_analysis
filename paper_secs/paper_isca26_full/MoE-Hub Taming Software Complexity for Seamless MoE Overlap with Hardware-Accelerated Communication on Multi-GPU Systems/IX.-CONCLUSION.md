# IX. CONCLUSION

In this paper, we propose MoE-Hub, a hardware-software co-design that addresses inefficiencies in distributed MoE models caused by inter-GPU communication bottlenecks. By decoupling data transmission from address management and hardware-accelerating the communication data flow, MoE-Hub enables seamless and high-performance overlap of computation and communication. MoE-Hub achieves 1.40×–3.08× speedup per MoE layer and 1.21×–1.98× end-to-end speedup over state-of-the-art works, offering a more efficient solution for large-scale MoE models.
